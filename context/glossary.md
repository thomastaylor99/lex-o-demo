## Brand names for speech recognition

Names a speech-to-text model tends to mishear. They seed `context_bias`; product names join once the catalogue exists.

- CPD: L'Oréal Paris, Garnier, Maybelline New York, NYX Professional Makeup, Essie
- Luxe: Lancôme, Yves Saint Laurent Beauté, Giorgio Armani Beauty, Kiehl's, Helena Rubinstein, Biotherm, Urban Decay, IT Cosmetics, Valentino Beauty, Prada Beauty, Mugler, Aēsop
- LDB: La Roche-Posay, CeraVe, Vichy, SkinCeuticals
- PPD: Kérastase, L'Oréal Professionnel, Redken, Matrix, Pureology

## Demo vocabulary

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| Perimeter | The set of real products the demo covers |
| Approved claim | A product claim quoted from the brand's own product page, stored with its source URL |
| Golden conversation | A scripted exchange replayed as text in tests to check tools, products and claims |
| Time to first audio | From the end of the visitor's speech to the first sound of the reply |
| Opener | The separate live voice-cloning segment that comes before this app |
