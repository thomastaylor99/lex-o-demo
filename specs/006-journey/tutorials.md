## How entries were found and checked

- Candidates came from YouTube search in English and French, searches inside the official channels of CeraVe (US, France), La Roche-Posay (France, UK and Ireland, USA, International, Canada, Arabia) and L'Oréal Paris (UK, France, USA), and web searches for TikTok links.
- Every link was checked on 2026-10-05 with the public oEmbed endpoints (`youtube.com/oembed`, `tiktok.com/oembed`). Links that returned no title or no author were dropped; YouTube refuses oEmbed for videos with embedding turned off, so those are out too.
- `title` is the oEmbed title as returned, and `creator` is the channel name (YouTube) or account handle (TikTok). After writing the file, all entries were checked again against oEmbed: every title and creator matches.
- The product match was checked from the title, the description and, when the title was vague, the thumbnail. Videos that compare with other companies' products, make medical claims or carry another company's sponsorship were left out. Paid partnerships with the product's own brand were kept.

## Points to check before approving

- CeraVe videos come from the US and Canada channels, which use US names (Moisturizing Cream, Hydrating Facial Cleanser, AM Facial Moisturizing Lotion with Sunscreen). The US AM lotion uses different sun filters from the UK SPF30 lotion; the way to apply it is the same. Dr. Daniel Sugai's review is also of the US lotion.
- No official brand video was found for Toleriane Sensitive Riche. The two creator shorts show the exact product (the Sonia Sami thumbnail shows the box) but come from small accounts (about 9,700 and 400 views).
- The La Roche-Posay France UVMune 400 video is a 6-second product ad.
- Doctor Anne's video reviews the UVMune 400 fluid alone, but her description lists affiliate links to other brands' sunscreens and discount codes for other skincare brands.
- Some creator videos are paid by the product's own brand: Dr. Daniel Sugai on TikTok (#CeraVePartner), Laura Ruth (gifted, affiliate links); Tom Allison's hashtags suggest a CeraVe partnership.
- beaute focus reviews all CeraVe cleansers sold in France, so it is listed under both cleansers. The raluca.makeup title does not say serum; the thumbnail shows the dropper serum being applied.
- Titles are stored exactly as oEmbed returns them, so several carry hashtags, emoji or capitals. The card may want to strip hashtags at display time.
