## Golden path

Visitor: dry, sensitive skin, likes rich textures, short routine, about 20 to 30 €, dry frizzy hair.

- Top pick: Toleriane Sensitive Riche. Alternatives: CeraVe Moisturising Cream, CeraVe SPF30 cream.
- Routine: CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser, plus Anthelios fragrance-free fluid for the day.
- Hair: Elseve Extraordinary Oil (the shampoo is an optional second step).
- Basket: cream and cleanser 21.62 €; with the oil 34.02 €; with the sunscreen 47.42 €. Two divisions.

Pairings (`pairs_with`): dry and sensitive (hydrating cleanser, Toleriane, Anthelios); oily (foaming cleanser, Oil Control gel-cream, Anthelios); radiance and first signs (cleanser, Vitamin C serum, Clinical SPF50+ fluid, Filler gel-cream at night); wrinkles on sensitive skin (hydrating cleanser, fragrance-free Revitalift, Anthelios).
